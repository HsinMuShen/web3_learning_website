Cryptocurrency is digital or virtual money secured by cryptography and built on blockchain technology, making it nearly impossible to counterfeit or double-spend.

## Simple Explanation

Cryptocurrency is money that only exists digitally. Unlike traditional money printed by governments, cryptocurrencies are created and managed by computer networks using complex mathematics and cryptography.

## Key Features

- **Digital**: Exists only in electronic form
- **Decentralized**: Not controlled by any government or bank
- **Secure**: Protected by cryptographic algorithms
- **Transparent**: All transactions are recorded publicly
- **Global**: Can be sent anywhere instantly

## Popular Cryptocurrencies

1. **Bitcoin (BTC)**: First and most valuable
2. **Ethereum (ETH)**: Programmable blockchain
3. **Stablecoins (USDC, USDT)**: Pegged to fiat currency
4. **Altcoins**: All other cryptocurrencies

## How to Get Cryptocurrency

- Buy on exchanges (Coinbase, Binance)
- Receive as payment
- Mine or stake
- Earn through DeFi protocols
