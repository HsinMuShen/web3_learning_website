A DAO (Decentralized Autonomous Organization) is an organization governed by code and controlled by its members rather than a central authority.

## Simple Explanation

Imagine a club where every decision is made by member voting, and all the rules are written in code that automatically executes. No president, no board of directors - just members and smart contracts.

## How It Works

1. **Members buy tokens** that give voting power
2. **Proposals are submitted** for changes or actions
3. **Members vote** using their tokens
4. **Smart contracts execute** approved decisions automatically

## Example

A DAO that manages a shared investment fund. Members vote on which projects to invest in, and the smart contract automatically sends funds to approved projects.

## Use Cases

- Investment funds
- Charity organizations
- Gaming guilds
- Protocol governance
