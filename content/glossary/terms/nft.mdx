NFT (Non-Fungible Token) is a unique digital asset that represents ownership of a specific item or piece of content on the blockchain.

## Simple Explanation

Unlike regular cryptocurrencies where every coin is identical (fungible), each NFT is unique and cannot be replaced with another. Think of it like owning the original Mona Lisa painting versus a copy - only one is the original.

## What Makes NFTs Special

- **Unique**: Each NFT has a distinct identifier
- **Ownership**: Blockchain proves you own it
- **Tradeable**: Can be bought, sold, or transferred
- **Programmable**: Can include royalties and special features

## Common Uses

- Digital art and collectibles
- Gaming items and characters
- Music and video content
- Virtual real estate
- Event tickets

## Example

An artist creates a digital artwork and mints it as an NFT. When you buy it, the blockchain records you as the owner, and you can prove authenticity and ownership.
