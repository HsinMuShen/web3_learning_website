A private key is a secret code that gives you access to your cryptocurrency. It's like a password that proves you own the assets in your wallet.

## Simple Explanation

Your private key is like the key to your house. Anyone who has this key can enter your house and take everything. That's why you must keep it completely secret and never share it with anyone.

## How It Works

- **You have a private key** (secret, only you know)
- **And a public key** (address others can see)
- **Private key signs transactions** proving you authorize them
- **Without private key**, you cannot access your crypto

## Critical Security Rules

⚠️ **Never share your private key** with anyone  
⚠️ **Never store it digitally** (avoid screenshots, emails)  
⚠️ **Write it down** and keep in a safe place  
⚠️ **No one legitimate** will ever ask for it

## Important

If you lose your private key, you lose access to your cryptocurrency forever. If someone else gets it, they can steal all your funds. There is no "forgot password" option.
