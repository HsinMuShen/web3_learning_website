A seed phrase (also called recovery phrase or mnemonic) is a series of 12-24 words that can restore access to your cryptocurrency wallet.

## Simple Explanation

Your seed phrase is like a master key to your entire wallet. If you lose your phone or computer, these words let you recover all your cryptocurrency. It's typically 12 or 24 random words like: "apple banana chair dog elephant..."

## Important Rules

⚠️ **Write it down** on paper (not digitally)  
⚠️ **Store securely** in a safe place  
⚠️ **Never share** with anyone  
⚠️ **Keep offline** (no photos, no cloud storage)  
⚠️ **Make backups** in multiple secure locations

## How It Works

When you create a wallet, it generates a seed phrase. This phrase mathematically derives all your private keys. Anyone with this phrase can recreate your entire wallet and access all funds.

## Example

If you lose your hardware wallet, you can buy a new one and enter your seed phrase to restore all your accounts and cryptocurrency.
