A cryptocurrency wallet is a digital tool that allows you to store, send, and receive cryptocurrencies. It doesn't actually store the coins themselves, but rather the keys to access them.

## Simple Explanation

Think of a wallet like a mailbox. The mailbox address (public key) is what others use to send you mail (crypto), and only you have the key (private key) to open it and access what's inside.

## Types of Wallets

- **Hot Wallets**: Connected to the internet (convenient but less secure)
- **Cold Wallets**: Offline storage (more secure but less convenient)
- **Hardware Wallets**: Physical devices for storing keys
- **Software Wallets**: Apps or programs on your computer or phone

## Important

Never share your private key or seed phrase with anyone. If someone has access to these, they can take all your cryptocurrency.
